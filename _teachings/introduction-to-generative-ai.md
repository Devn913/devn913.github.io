## Course Overview

Introduction to Generative AI was a 6-month course delivered to 2nd year engineering students at ADYPU (January - May 2026). The course progressed from foundational machine learning to modern generative AI systems:

- Linear Regression
- Logistic Regression
- Neural Networks
- Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
- Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)
- NLP Fundamentals
- Transformers
- Agentic AI (LangGraph, LangChain) including no-code tools like n8n
